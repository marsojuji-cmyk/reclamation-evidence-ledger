We Publish Our Misses Transparency In Action

Audit Record: The Version 1 Correction

When our initial aggregate seasonal median called all 27 first-run pilot sites "recovering", we did not sweep the anomalous finding under the rug. We documented the phenology sampling trap, printed the correction, rebuilt the engine to require month-matched comparison, and published the whole audit on the ledger.

Under synthetic benchmark validation, injected traces at +0.30, +0.01, and −0.30 were correctly detected, while the seasonal phenology trap was correctly REFUSED.

Every analysis packet published on the ledger links directly to its source chips and provides an explicit refusal statement when data fails our confidence thresholds.
