Reclamation Evidence is a screening service for reclamation and closure programs. We rank disturbed sites by vegetation recovery signal from Sentinel-2 satellite imagery so field crews go first to the sites that need eyes. Screening and triage only — we never certify reclamation, prove contamination, or replace field assessment. Every claim we make is published alongside its misses.

How It Operates Three-Step Protocol

Step 1 · Signal Ranking

1. We rank your sites by recovery signal.

Using month-matched Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery across historical growing seasons, we calculate median-of-deltas across vegetation indices. Sites exhibiting stagnation or negative trajectories rise to the top of the walk register.

Step 2 · Blind Validation

2. We validate blind against your field data.

Under a pre-registered protocol, our automated triage ranking is compared against your ground assessment records without seeing the labels in advance. Uncertainty is quantified and reported in writing.

Step 3 · Provenance & Misses

3. We publish the method and the misses.

Every analysis packet provides complete radiometric lineage, cloud masks, raw chip checksums, and explicit refusal notices. We print our errors and self-audits openly.

The Boundary Line · What We Do Not Do

  • No compliance or reclamation certification: Satellite screening prioritizes inspection; it never issues a regulatory stamp.
  • No contamination status or attribution: 10 m pixels cannot prove chemical presence or identify responsible parties.
  • No replacement for qualified field judgment: Screening directs finite field capacity to the highest-priority acreage.
