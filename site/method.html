Scientific Tradecraft & Calibration

Month-Matched Orbital Screening

Greenness follows the calendar, not the project. To prevent seasonal vegetation cycles from mimicking reclamation progress, our screening engine compares every observation month-for-month against historical baselines.

The Core Triad Interpretive Boundaries

Borrowed from imagery interpretation tradecraft, our pipeline enforces strict epistemic boundaries:

Tier Formal Definition System Authority
Detection A measured spectral change in vegetation indices (NDVI/NDMI/SAVI/MSAVI) relative to baseline. Automated algorithmic output.
Identification The observed change is consistent with sustained vegetation recovery across multiple seasons (delta ≥ +0.08, zero not crossed). Automated hypothesis with caveats.
Attribution Assigning legal causation or naming a responsible operator for observed conditions. Never emitted by code. Requires human ground truth.

Uncertainty Quantification Statistical Rigor

The Crossing-Zero Rule & Missing Intervals

A point estimate without uncertainty is an invitation to confirmation bias. When at least 4 matched calendar months are available, we compute a 95% bootstrap confidence interval over resampled month-matched deltas (1,000 resamples, deterministic seed).

The crossing-zero rule: If the 95% confidence interval crosses zero, recovery identification is refused, regardless of how positive the point estimate appears.

Honest disclosure: With fewer than 4 matched months, the resampled median distribution degenerates into trivial clusters that give a false impression of precision. Rather than reporting a misleading interval, we report no interval at all. In our 99-site pilot, exactly 12 sites carry no interval because they had fewer than 4 matched months.

The Caught Phenology Mistake

A watchdog that cannot catch its own errors cannot be trusted to evaluate anything else.

Our v1 method compared aggregate seasonal vegetation medians between baseline and current years. That initial pipeline reported that all 27 pilot sites were recovering.

A rigorous self-audit discovered the error: baseline imagery had been acquired primarily during May and June, whereas the assessment period was sampled heavily in July. Summer in Alberta is greener than spring. The algorithm was measuring the calendar, not reclamation.

We printed the correction publicly, rebuilt the entire architecture around month-matched median-of-deltas with a hard refusal rule (minimum 2 matched months), and published the whole revision to the public ledger.

Honest Limits of Sentinel-2 Screening

  • 10-metre resolution: Individual wellheads, pumps, and localized contamination plumes cannot be resolved.
  • Growing-season boundary: Comparisons are valid only May through September. Winter snow cover (November–March) makes multispectral diffs meaningless.
  • Drought sensitivity: Severe regional drought suppresses vegetation indices regardless of reclamation work; baseline comparisons absorb regional context.
  • Zero regulatory authority: Orbital signals are triage evidence for ground deployment, never legal certifications.
