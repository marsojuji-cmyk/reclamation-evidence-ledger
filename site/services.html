What We Do Not Do · Full Statement of Limits

Stating our boundaries clearly is what makes our evidence defensible:

  • No compliance or reclamation certification: We provide screening and triage. We never certify legal reclamation or issue compliance approvals.
  • No contamination status, causation, responsibility, or attribution: 10-metre multispectral pixels cannot prove soil chemistry, groundwater conditions, or assign blame.
  • No replacement for field assessment or qualified professional judgment: Satellite data directs personnel; only qualified professionals can verify ground truth.
