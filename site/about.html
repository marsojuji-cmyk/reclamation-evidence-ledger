About Reclamation Evidence

Founded in Calgary, Alberta, Reclamation Evidence builds independent remote-sensing tooling to bring empirical clarity to closure programs.

The Core Thesis Operational Philosophy

Field Capacity is Finite

Alberta has tens of thousands of inactive and orphan oil and gas wells. Environmental reclamation specialists, agrologists, and field technicians have finite hours in the short northern growing season.

Screening earns field days: Sending expensive field crews to sites that are either thriving or buried under seasonal grass changes wastes resources. Remote screening ranks sites with cold mathematical honesty, directing field boots to the pads that show stagnation, erosion, or unexpected regression.

Provenance-first: In an era of black-box algorithms and synthetic marketing claims, we publish every chip, every formula, and every statistical miss. A claim without provenance is not evidence.
